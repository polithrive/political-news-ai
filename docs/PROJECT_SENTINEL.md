# PoliticalPulse

## Mission

People don't need more political news.

They need better tools to understand it.

PoliticalPulse uses artificial intelligence to transform political news into structured intelligence reports that help readers understand issues from multiple perspectives without telling them what to believe.

---

# Vision

Become the world's most trusted AI-powered political intelligence platform.

Not another news website.

Not another AI chatbot.

An intelligence platform that helps people understand complex political issues.

---

# Core Principles

## Neutral

Never advocate for political positions.

## Transparent

Separate facts from interpretation.

## Explain

Always explain why something matters.

## Compare

Present multiple viewpoints fairly.

## Consensus

Highlight where perspectives agree.

## Intelligence

Help users understand, not react.

---

# Success Metrics

Users should be able to understand a political story in under five minutes.

Users should leave with more understanding than when they arrived.

PoliticalPulse should reduce confusion, not increase it.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Become the default place people visit when they want to understand political issues objectively.

# PoliticalPulse AI Methodology

## Overview

PoliticalPulse does not simply summarize news articles.

PoliticalPulse analyzes evidence from multiple sources to help users understand complex political issues.

The Intelligence Engine follows a structured methodology designed to maximize transparency and minimize unsupported conclusions.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Evidence

PoliticalPulse collects reporting from multiple news organizations whenever possible.

This includes the primary article selected by the user and related reporting covering the same event.

---

## Step 2 — Rank Sources

Each source is evaluated using internal metadata including:

- Reliability
- Factual reporting history
- Political perspective
- Source diversity

This metadata helps the Intelligence Engine understand the reporting landscape.

---

## Step 3 — Compare Reporting

The engine identifies:

- Shared facts
- Unique claims
- Missing context
- Areas of disagreement
- Areas of agreement

---

## Step 4 — Measure Confidence

PoliticalPulse estimates confidence using:

- Number of independent sources
- Source quality
- Reporting consistency
- Available evidence

Confidence reflects the available reporting—not certainty.

---

## Step 5 — Generate Intelligence

The final report includes:

- Executive Summary
- Why This Matters
- Key Facts
- Perspective Analysis
- Consensus
- Fact Check
- Evidence
- Timeline
- Intelligence Graph

---

## Guiding Principles

PoliticalPulse aims to:

- Explain rather than persuade.
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Communicate uncertainty honestly.
- Present multiple perspectives fairly.
- Continuously improve through evidence.
